The 4-Stage Lens Precision Pipeline

01

Optical Sorting

Separated into single vision, bifocal, progressive, reading glasses, and sunglasses.

02

Ultrasonic Sanitization

Cleaned in multi-stage sanitizing baths to ensure hospital-grade hygiene.

03

Lensometer Reading

Trained volunteers measure and verify exact sphere, cylinder, and axis on precision lensometers.

04

Mission Packaging

Individually labeled, indexed in inventory, and boxed for overseas medical teams.

Our Story & Leadership

District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center was chartered in 2014 to ensure that cost and geography never prevent a person from receiving proper refractive vision care.

Core Principles

  • 🔹 Integrity: Honest stewardship of every donated pair of glasses and funding dollar.
  • 🔹 Compassion: Unwavering commitment to vulnerable individuals worldwide.
  • 🔹 Environmental Sustainability: Rescuing reusable frames from landfills.
  • 🔹 Civic Community: Engaging students, scouts, Lions Clubs, and corporate teams.
  • 🔹 Clinical Accuracy: Rigorous optical quality checks on all lens distributions.

👓 Acceptance Guidelines

Accepted Donations & Technical Services

We process eyeglasses, laptops, mobile phones, and hearing aids. Learn what items we accept and how to donate.

👓

Eyeglasses & Sunglasses

All prescription glasses, readers, bifocals, and sunglasses in any condition, including broken frames.

💻

Laptops & Tablets

Windows 11 compatible or installed laptops and tablets only. (No desktop computer towers).

📱

Cell Phones

Smartphones and feature phones in any condition. Data is wiped completely prior to refurbishment.

🔊

Hearing Aids

Functional or non-functional hearing devices repurposed with professional audiology partners.

⚠️ Items We Cannot Accept

Damaged or shattered laptop screens, missing laptop keyboards, desktop towers, and home electronics. For bulk or unusual donations, please contact us prior to delivery.
