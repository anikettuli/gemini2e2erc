Our Mission & Global Reach

Our Mission: The District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center will provide eyeglasses, when available and without cost, to those missions who have a method of determining the refractive status of the eye.

Our recycled glasses are available for Humanitarian and Medical Missions around the world.

Our 4-Step Recycling Process

We have a dedicated process to ensure used eyeglasses are ready for a new owner.

Step 1: Sorting

Eyeglasses are sorted into categories, such as non-prescription, nearsightedness (minus), farsightedness (plus), bifocal, trifocal, progressive, and prism.

Step 2: Washing & Drying

Glasses are thoroughly cleaned and sanitized to ensure they're ready for their new owner.

Step 3: Measuring the Prescription

We use specialized equipment to determine the exact prescription of each lens.

Step 4: Final Sorting & Storage

The processed glasses are sorted one last time and stored, ready for distribution.

About Lions District 2-E2 ERC

A community-driven organization dedicated to recycling eyeglasses and serving global mission partners.

Our Story

District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center was founded in 2013 and officially recognized in 2014 as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We were created with a simple vision: to ensure that no one should go without proper vision care due to cost or access limitations.

What started as a small group of passionate volunteers has grown into a thriving organization that serves mission partners across 40+ countries. Every single member of our team is a volunteer, dedicated to making a global impact.

Our Services

Complete eyeglass recycling solutions for individuals and organizations

What We Accept

👓 Eyeglasses

We accept all eyeglasses including sunglasses in any condition. Even broken.

💻 Laptops & Computers

We only accept laptops or tablets that are Windows 11 ready or already installed. No desktops.

📱 Cell Phones

Any brand or model: Smartphones and basic phones in any condition. Data will be completely wiped.

🔊 Hearing Aids

Functional or non-functional: We work with partners to ensure these devices reach those who need them.

How Our Process Works

Our 5-Step Process
1. Collection Items collected from donors
2. Inspection We inspect and assess all items
3. Processing Cleaning, sorting, organization
4. Quality Check Final quality assurance review
5. Distribution Shipped to mission partners

Items We Cannot Accept

Note: For safety and quality reasons, we cannot accept damaged screens, non-functional keyboards, or items with missing parts. Call (817) 710-5403 if you have questions.
