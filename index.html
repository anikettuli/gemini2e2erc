The Need for Clear Vision

According to the World Health Organization, at least 2.2 billion people globally have a near or distance vision impairment. Of these, over 1 billion cases remain completely unaddressed. A simple pair of glasses can change everything:

👧 Children

Education, developmental milestones, and reading ability.

💼 Adults

Employment opportunities, trade work, and economic independence.

👴 Seniors

Mobility, independence, and active engagement with family.

🌍 Communities

Reduced poverty, higher literacy, and collective resilience.

Our 4-Step Recycling Process

01

Sorting

Categorized by frame type, single vision, bifocal, trifocal, progressive, readers, and sunglasses.

02

Washing & Sanitizing

Ultrasonic cleaning baths sanitize and prepare frames and lenses for their next owner.

03

Lensometer Measurement

Volunteers use precision lensometers to record exact optical sphere, cylinder, and axis.

04

Mission Packing

Individually labeled, bagged, and boxed for medical missions heading around the world.

📖 Organization Background • Est. 2013

Our Story & Leadership

Founded in 2013 and chartered in 2014 as an official 501(c)(3) nonprofit, the District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center was built on the premise that no human being should lack clear vision due to geographic or financial limitations.

Our Mission & Values

The District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center provides eyeglasses without cost to medical and humanitarian missions who possess clinical methods for determining the refractive status of the eye.

  • 🔹 Integrity: Honest stewardship of every donated frame and dollar.
  • 🔹 Compassion: Dedicated service to vulnerable populations globally.
  • 🔹 Sustainability: Keeping millions of usable items out of municipal landfills.
  • 🔹 Community: Empowering corporate, youth, and civic volunteers.
  • 🔹 Excellence: Strict optical measurement standards for clinical mission teams.

📦 Recycling & Acceptance Standards

Accepted Donations & Services

We accept a variety of medical and technology items. Every item processed by our volunteers directly equips missions or helps fund operations.

👓

Eyeglasses & Sunglasses

All prescription eyeglasses, reading glasses, bifocals, and sunglasses in any condition, including broken frames.

💻

Laptops & Tablets

Windows 11 ready or pre-installed laptops and tablets only. (No desktop tower computers accepted).

📱

Cell Phones

Smartphones and basic phones of any brand in any condition. All user data is thoroughly wiped prior to reuse.

🔊

Hearing Aids

Functional or non-functional hearing aids. Repurposed in partnership with hearing health professionals.

⚠️ Items We Cannot Accept: Broken or shattered laptop screens, missing laptop keyboards, desktop computer towers, and household appliances. For questions, call (817) 710-5403.

How It Works: 5-Stage Lifecycle

  1. Collection: Picked up from 150+ partner drop-off boxes across North Texas.
  2. Initial Inspection: Assessed for lens viability and frame integrity.
  3. Processing: Washed, sanitized, and classified by category.
  4. Quality Check: Measured with automated and manual lensometers.
  5. Global Distribution: Packaged and handed off to verified mission trips.

Drop-Off Locations FAQ

Do partner locations have the same hours as the main center?
No, each partner location (such as libraries, optical shops, and community centers) operates on their own business hours. Please verify their operating schedule before visiting.
Can I drop off laptops or computers at partner locations?
Drop-off boxes in partner locations are sized primarily for eyeglasses, cell phones, and hearing aids. For Windows 11 laptops and tablets, please bring them directly to the main center on Saturdays between 9 AM and 3 PM.
