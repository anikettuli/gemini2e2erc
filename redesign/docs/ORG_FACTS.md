## 2. Mission, History & Quantitative Impact

### Mission & Program Rules
- **Purpose:** Collect, inspect, clean, measure, sort, pack, and supply refurbished eyeglasses — **when available and without cost** — to non-profit humanitarian and medical missions that have a method for determining the refractive status of the eye.
- **Distribution Scope:** Supplies single-vision prescription eyeglasses and sunglasses for medical missions; can supply reading glasses when eye-care professionals are unavailable (`files/ERC-Brochure.pdf`).
- **Mission Conditions:** Glasses are strictly **not for resale**; requesting mission organizations are responsible for all **shipping expenses** (`files/Mission-Request-Form.pdf`).

### Global Need & Unit Economics
- According to the **World Health Organization (WHO)**: at least **2.2 billion** people globally have a near or distance vision impairment, of whom at least **1 billion** cases remain unaddressed.
- Per `files/ERC-Brochure.pdf`: more than **157 million** people worldwide suffer from poor eyesight simply due to lack of glasses.
- **Processing Cost:** Less than **$0.10 US (`10¢ US`)** to process and provide a pair of recycled eyeglasses.

### History & Impact Milestones
- **2013:** Lions District 2-E2 Eyeglass Recycling Center founded.
- **March 20 & April 12, 2014:** Corporate Bylaws adopted and ratified (`files/ERC-Bylaws.pdf`).
- **2014:** Officially recognized as a **501(c)(3)** nonprofit organization.
- **Cumulative Reach:** Hundreds of thousands of pairs repurposed; **40+ countries** served through partner eye clinics and humanitarian missions.
- **Record Expansion:** In **2023–2024**, the center supplied **17,000+** pairs for foreign missions (`17,211` foreign + `1,850` local per brochure); in **2024–2025**, it shipped **`17,692` foreign** + **`4,004` local** pairs.

## 3. Accepted Donations & Refurbishment Workflow

### Accepted Item Categories
1. **Eyeglasses (Core Collection):**
   - All new and used **prescription eyeglasses**, **reading glasses**, and **sunglasses** in **any condition** — including **broken** glasses or frames missing parts (repaired or repurposed).
   - *Data gap note:* Eyeglass **cases** and loose **metal frames** are not separately enumerated in source documents beyond "eyeglasses including sunglasses in any condition, even broken."
2. **Hearing Aids:**
   - **Digital or analog** hearing aids, **functional or non-functional** (processed with specialized partners to reach recipients in need).
3. **Laptops & Tablets:**
   - Accepted **ONLY if Windows 11–ready or already running Windows 11**.
   - **Desktop computers are NOT accepted.** *(Note: One brief FAQ sentence generically says "laptops, computers", while the explicit electronics acceptance policy specifies Windows 11-ready/installed laptops and tablets only and explicitly rejects desktops.)*
4. **Cell Phones:**
   - Smartphones and basic mobile phones of **any brand or model**, in **any condition**.
   - All device data is **completely wiped**.

### Explicitly Not Accepted
- **Desktop computers**
- **Damaged screens**
- **Non-functional keyboards**
- **Electronics or items with missing parts**
- **Partner Bin Variance:** Individual DFW drop-off bin locations may accept only eyeglasses or a subset of electronics — donors should verify acceptable items with the specific partner location before visiting or call `(817) 710-5403`.

### Bulk, Shipping, Host Boxes & Monetary Donations
- **Business & Large Organization Donations:** Welcomed; call `(817) 710-5403` to coordinate delivery or pickup.
- **Shipped Donations (Large Quantities):** Email `2e2erc1854@gmail.com` before shipping to receive shipping instructions or coordinate pickup.
- **Sponsor / Host Collection Boxes:** Businesses and organizations can request collection boxes via email, phone, or the center contact flow. Retail hosts cited include **Westlake Ace Hardware** and **MarketStreet**.
- **Monetary Donations:** Make checks payable/mail to `Lions District 2-E2 ERC, P.O. Box 1854, Keller, TX 76244`. **100%** of financial contributions support mission shipping, processing supplies, and facility operations. Tax-deductible under 501(c)(3) status per donor's tax preparer.

### Refurbishment & Dispatch Workflow
Source documentation describes both a **4-step recycling flow** and a **5-step operational lifecycle** (`index.html`), plus specific packaging specifications (`files/ERC-Brochure.pdf`). Combined factually end-to-end:
1. **Collection:** Gather donated items from `214` listed DFW drop-off boxes, Lions Clubs, corporate donors, and walk-in/mailed contributions.
2. **Inspection & Initial Sorting:** Assess usability and separate eyeglasses into optical categories:
   - Non-prescription / sunglasses
   - Reading glasses
   - Nearsighted (minus / concave)
   - Farsighted (plus / convex)
   - Bifocal, trifocal, progressive, and prism lenses
3. **Washing, Sanitizing & Drying:** Clean frames and lenses thoroughly, sanitize, and dry.
4. **Prescription Measurement (Lensometry):** Determine exact lens prescription using lensometers.
5. **Final Quality Check, Tagging & Boxed Storage:** Inspect final quality, label/bag, and pack into standardized **50-pair boxes by 0.25-diopter increment**:
   - **Single Vision Plus (`+`):** `+0.25` to `+5.00+` (`50 pairs per box`)
   - **Single Vision Minus (`-`):** `-0.25` to `-5.00+` (`50 pairs per box`)
   - **Reading Glasses (`+`):** `+0.25` to `+5.00+` (`50 pairs per box`)
   - **Non-prescription Sunglasses:** Boxes of `50 pairs`
6. **Mission Dispatch:** Fulfill verified mission orders and ship free of hardware cost (mission covers freight) to nonprofit clinics worldwide.

## 5. Volunteering, Group Sessions & Visitor FAQs

### Individual Volunteering
- **Walk-Ins Welcome:** Individuals may drop in during Saturday open hours (`9:00 AM - 3:00 PM`) or register online for open sessions such as **2nd Saturday Open Session** (typically `12:00 PM - 3:00 PM`, online registration cap `15` volunteers).
- **Typical Tasks:** Sorting and organizing donations, washing and drying eyeglasses, measuring prescriptions (lensometry), packing mission boxes/shipments, and administrative support.
- **Prior Experience:** None required; training happens on-site.

### Group Volunteer Days
- **Capacity:** Up to **20 volunteers per group** per organized session.
- **Standard Shift Windows Seen in Schedule:** Morning `9:00 AM - 12:00 PM` and Afternoon `12:00 PM - 3:00 PM`.
- **Eligible Groups:** Corporate teams, schools, civic clubs, Lions Clubs, National Charity League (NCL) chapters, Scouting troops (BSA / Eagle Scouts), SASO chapters, church youth groups, and community organizations.
- **How to Schedule:** Call `(817) 710-5403`, email `2e2erc1854@gmail.com`, register for an open slot, or submit **`files/Group-Volunteer-Form.pdf`** by email to `2e2erc1854@gmail.com`.

### Documented Policy Omissions (Do Not Fabricate)
- A complete review of all website files, event records, and downloadable PDFs confirms that **no minimum volunteer age**, **no adult-to-youth chaperone ratio**, and **no closed-toe shoe / dress-code requirements** are specified in organizational documentation. Builders must **not** invent age limits, chaperone ratios, or attire mandates.

### Verified Factual Q&A Reference
- **What items can be donated?** Eyeglasses (any condition, including sunglasses and broken frames), hearing aids (digital/analog, working or non-working), Windows 11-ready/installed laptops & tablets (no desktops), and cell phones (smartphones/basic, data wiped). Damaged screens, non-functional keyboards, and electronics with missing parts are not accepted. Partner drop-off locations vary in what they accept.
- **What are the operating hours?** Most Saturdays `9:00 AM - 3:00 PM`; weekday (`Mon-Fri`) drop-offs or appointments by calling `(817) 710-5403` or emailing; closed Sundays.
- **Can items be dropped off on weekdays?** Yes, by appointment (`(817) 710-5403` or `2e2erc1854@gmail.com`).
- **Are damaged eyeglasses accepted?** Yes — glasses in any condition, even broken, can often be repaired or repurposed.
- **Are bulk/business donations accepted?** Yes; call `(817) 710-5403` to coordinate delivery or pickup.
- **How can large quantities be shipped?** Email `2e2erc1854@gmail.com` before shipping to arrange instructions or pickup.
- **Are donations tax-deductible?** Lions District 2-E2 ERC is a recognized `501(c)(3)` organization; donations may be tax-deductible (check with a tax preparer).
- **How are eyeglasses used in missions?** Partnering with missionaries and non-profit organizations across `40+` countries to distribute refurbished eyewear free of charge.
